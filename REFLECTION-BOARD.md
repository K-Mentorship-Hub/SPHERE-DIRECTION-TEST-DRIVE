## Red / Yellow / Green Flags

### Green flags (strong fit signals)
- You forgot to check the time
- You went beyond the task requirements
- You wanted to show someone your result
- The problems felt personally meaningful
- You're already thinking about what to build next

### Yellow flags (possible fit, needs more data)
- Some parts were engaging, others were tedious
- You liked the idea but not the daily work
- You did it well but didn't enjoy it
- You'd do it if paid, but not for fun

### Red flags (probably not your direction)
- You couldn't wait to finish
- You felt no curiosity about going deeper
- The problems felt irrelevant to you
- You were bored even when the task was easy

> **Remember:** No result is wrong. A red flag is just as valuable as a green one — it saves you years on the wrong path.

## What Is This?

This repo is the **career test-drive layer** of [K Mentorship Hub](https://github.com/K-Mentorship-Hub).

It gives you a structured way to:

- **try** a direction before committing years to it
- **feel** what the daily work is actually like
- **decide** whether it fits your personality, skills, and values

No courses. No certificates. Just **real micro-experiences** that tell you more than any job description.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1: The 7-Day Challenge

Start with the [7-Day Challenge](./7-DAY-CHALLENGE.md) — three tiny experiments from three different directions. By the end of the week, you'll know more about yourself than hours of career quizzes could tell you.

### Step 2: Direction Mini-Quests

Found a direction that felt right? Dive deeper with a [direction mini-quest](./QUESTS/). Each quest is a 2-4 hour hands-on experience that simulates real work in that field.

### Step 3: Reflect & Decide

Use the [Reflection Board](./REFLECTION-BOARD.md) to capture what you learned. Then choose: go deeper, try another direction, or move on.

## Quest Structure

Every mini-quest follows the same format:

1. **Scenario** — you're given a realistic role and situation
2. **Theory bite** — just enough context to understand what you're doing
3. **Hands-on task** — a real micro-project (not a quiz)
4. **Reflection questions** — what did this feel like? would you do this daily?
5. **Verdict** — green / yellow / red flag indicators

---

## How To Read Your Results

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| You forgot to check the time | This direction engages you naturally |
| You wanted to keep going after the task | Strong fit signal |
| You felt bored or frustrated | Might not be your thing — or the task was too easy/hard |
| You googled things on your own | Genuine curiosity — great sign |
| You couldn't wait to finish | This direction is probably not for you |

**No result is wrong.** The point is to know, not to guess.

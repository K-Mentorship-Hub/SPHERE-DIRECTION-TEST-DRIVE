# 7-Day Challenge

**Three directions. Seven days. One answer: what fits me best now?**

---

## The Idea

Career quizzes tell you what you *might* like. This challenge tells you what you *actually* like — by making you do real micro-tasks from three different directions.

You won't read about careers. You'll **simulate** them.

---

## The Rules

1. Pick **3 directions** from the [14 directions](./README.md#the-14-directions) — ideally from different spheres (S, E, T, Bridge)
2. Spend **2 days** on each direction (Day 1-2, 3-4, 5-6)
3. Day 7: **reflect** and decide
4. Be honest — if something bored you, write that down
5. Don't skip the reflection — it's the most important part

---

## Day 1-2: Direction A — ___________

Open the mini-quest for your first direction and complete it.

**During the task, notice:**
- Did time fly or drag?
- Did you google things on your own?
- Did you want to show someone what you made?
- Did you feel overwhelmed or energized?

<details>
<summary>Not sure which 3 directions to pick?</summary>

Try one from each sphere:

| Sphere | Pick one | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| T | Quest 2 (Code & Engineering) or Quest 3 (Cyber & Infra) | Most people curious about tech start here |
| E | Quest 4 (Digital Product & Growth) | Shows if you think in terms of users and markets |
| S | Quest 7 (Green / Sustainability) or Quest 6 (Medicine / Bio) | Tests if domain problems engage you |
| Bridge | Quest 5 (Design & UX) or Quest 14 (People / Human Factor) | Tests if you think across boundaries |

Pick the three that sound most interesting — not the ones that sound most impressive.

</details>

---

## Day 3-4: Direction B — ___________

Open the mini-quest for your second direction and complete it.

**Same questions, different field:**
- Was this easier or harder than Direction A?
- Did different skills come naturally?
- Did the problems feel meaningful to you?

---

## Day 5-6: Direction C — ___________

Open the mini-quest for your third direction and complete it.

**Now compare all three:**
- Which one made you forget to check your phone?
- Which one felt like "work" vs "play"?
- Which result are you most proud of?

---

## Day 7: Reflection Board

Fill out the [Reflection Board](./REFLECTION-BOARD.md) with your honest answers.

Then answer the one question that matters:

### **What fits me best now?**

Write your answer below. It doesn't have to be permanent. It just has to be honest.

```
My best-fit direction right now: __________________

Because:
- _______________________________________________
- _______________________________________________
- _______________________________________________

I want to explore further: ________________________

I'm probably not suited for: ______________________
```

---

## What Happens Next

- **Strong fit?** Go to the relevant [Sphere repo](https://github.com/K-Mentorship-Hub) and start practice tasks
- **Tie between two?** Do a second mini-quest for both — the tiebreaker is how you *feel*, not what scores higher
- **Nothing fit?** That's valuable data too. Try directions 12-14 (general development, language, people) — they might be your bridge
- **Ready to build?** Move to MVP Lab in the relevant sphere

---

## Time Investment

| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|----------|------|
| 1-2 | Direction A mini-quest | 2-4 hours |
| 3-4 | Direction B mini-quest | 2-4 hours |
| 5-6 | Direction C mini-quest | 2-4 hours |
| 7 | Reflection & decision | 1 hour |
| **Total** | | **~7-13 hours over 7 days** |

You can spread this across evenings or weekends. The point is consistency, not speed.

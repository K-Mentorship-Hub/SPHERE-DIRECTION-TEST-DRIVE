# Quest 13 — Language Learning

**Sphere:** Bridge | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

You need to reach professional-level English (or another target language) within 12 months for a career opportunity. Design your own learning system — no expensive courses, just strategy and consistency.

---

## Theory Bite

Effective language learning is a **systems problem**, not a talent problem. Key principles:

- Comprehensible input > grammar drills (Krashen)
- Spaced repetition beats massed practice
- Output (speaking/writing) accelerates acquisition
- Context-based vocabulary > random word lists
- Consistency (15 min/day) > intensity (3 hours once a week)

The meta-skill: **designing a sustainable rhythm that compounds over time.**

---

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: Level Assessment (15 min)

Rate your current level in your target language (CEFR scale):

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| A1 | Can understand basic phrases |
| A2 | Can communicate in simple routines |
| B1 | Can deal with most travel/work situations |
| B2 | Can interact with fluency and spontaneity |
| C1 | Can express ideas flexibly for social/academic purposes |
| C2 | Near-native mastery |

Where are you now? Where do you need to be?

### Part 2: Input Plan (30 min)

Design your daily input:
- **Listening:** 15 min (podcast, YouTube, Netflix with subtitles)
- **Reading:** 15 min (news, articles, graded readers)
- **Vocabulary:** 10 new words/day via spaced repetition (Anki, Quizlet)

List specific resources you'd use for each.

### Part 3: Output Plan (30 min)

Design your weekly output:
- **Writing:** 3x/week, 100 words (journal, Reddit comments, email)
- **Speaking:** 2x/week, 15 min (language exchange, shadowing, voice notes)

How will you get feedback on your output?

### Part 4: Context Vocabulary System (45 min)

Pick one professional domain (tech, business, science). Create a vocabulary tracker:

| Word | Context sentence | Domain | Review count |
|------|-----------------|--------|-------------|
| deploy | "We deployed the update at 3am" | Tech | 0 |
| validation | "The validation step caught the error" | Tech/Business | 0 |

Collect 20 domain-specific words with context sentences.

## Reflection Questions

- Did you enjoy designing the system more than the language practice itself?
- Is the consistency challenge exciting or daunting?
- Do you see language as a tool or as a passion?

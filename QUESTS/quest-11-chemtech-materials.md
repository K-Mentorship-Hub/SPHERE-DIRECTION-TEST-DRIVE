# Quest 11 — ChemTech & Materials

**Sphere:** S | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

A startup is developing a new battery chemistry for energy storage. They need someone to research the landscape, compare approaches, and write a non-specialist explainer for their investors.

---

## Theory Bite

Materials science and chemical technology are about **understanding and engineering matter at the molecular level**. Key concepts:

- Materials properties (strength, conductivity, reactivity) determine applications
- The gap between lab discovery and commercial product is often 10+ years
- Energy storage is one of the biggest material science challenges of our time
- Sustainability is driving demand for new materials (biodegradable, recyclable, lower-carbon)

### Part 2: Non-Specialist Explainer (45 min)

Write a 1-page explainer for investors:
- Why does battery chemistry matter?
- What's the current state of the art?
- What would a breakthrough mean?
- What are the risks?

No jargon. If you must use a technical term, define it inline.

### Part 3: Application Map (30 min)

Map where each battery type is best suited:
- Electric vehicles
- Grid storage
- Consumer electronics
- Aerospace

Why doesn't one battery type rule them all?

### Part 4: Research Frontier Note (30 min)

Find one recent development in battery/materials science (news article or paper summary). Write 3 bullet points:
- What was discovered?
- Why does it matter?
- What's still needed before it's commercial?

# Quest 12 — General Development

**Sphere:** Bridge | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

You're between directions and not sure what to learn next. Instead of picking randomly, build a personal learning system that helps you explore efficiently and decide with data, not vibes.

---

## Theory Bite

General development is about **meta-skills** — learning how to learn, building systems for yourself, and creating structure where there is none. Key concepts:

- Deliberate practice beats passive consumption
- Spaced repetition beats cramming
- Output-based learning beats input-only (make things, don't just watch tutorials)
- A learning system is more valuable than any single course

This direction is for people who want to **get better at getting better**.

---

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: Learning Audit (30 min)

List everything you've tried to learn in the last year:
- What stuck? Why?
- What didn't stick? Why not?
- What was your best learning session? What made it work?

### Part 2: Build a Learning System (60 min)

Design your personal learning system. Include:

- **Input sources** (books, courses, tutorials, people)
- **Practice method** (how will you apply what you learn?)
- **Output format** (notes, projects, blog posts, teaching)
- **Review rhythm** (daily, weekly, monthly checkpoints)
- **Anti-chaos rules** (3 rules to prevent overwhelm)

Write it as a one-page document you could actually follow.

### Part 3: 7-Day Learning Sprint (plan only — 30 min)

Pick one skill you want to test. Plan a 7-day sprint:
- Day 1-2: Fundamentals (what are the 5 most important concepts?)
- Day 3-4: First output (build the smallest possible thing)
- Day 5-6: Deepen (fix what's broken, add one feature)
- Day 7: Reflect (was this skill worth pursuing?)

### Part 4: Focus Rhythm Planner (30 min)

Create a daily template for focused work:

| Block | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Morning | 90 min | Deep practice |
| Midday | 30 min | Review & notes |
| Afternoon | 60 min | Build / output |
| Evening | 15 min | Plan tomorrow |

Adjust to your schedule. The key is **consistency over intensity**.

---

## Reflection Questions

- Did designing systems for yourself feel natural?
- Do you prefer structure or freedom in how you learn?
- Could you be the kind of person who helps others learn, not just yourself?

## Verdict

| If this describes you | Flag |
|-----------------------|------|
| You kept refining the system after the task | Green |
| You want to share this system with others | Green |
| You wanted someone to give you a system, not build one | Yellow (maybe more structured paths) |
| Building systems felt like procrastination from actual learning | Yellow |
| Meta-learning doesn't interest you — you want to DO things | Red (pick a specific direction!) |

# Quest 4 — Digital Product & Growth

**Sphere:** E | **Time:** 2-4 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

You're a product-minded person who just joined a friend's startup. They built an app but nobody is using it. Your job: figure out who the users really are, how to reach them, and what to say to make them care.

---

## Theory Bite

Digital product and growth is about **connecting a product to the people who need it**. The core loop:

1. **Understand** who your users are and what they need
2. **Position** your product — why is it different/better?
3. **Message** it in a way that resonates (not just features, but outcomes)
4. **Distribute** through the right channels
5. **Measure** what works and double down

Daily reality: lots of research, writing, testing messages, reading analytics, and making decisions with incomplete data.

Key truth: **building the product is 20% of the work. Getting people to use it is 80%.**

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: User Persona (30 min)

Pick a real or imaginary product (e.g., a habit-tracking app). Create one user persona:

- **Name:** e.g., "Busy Bethany"
- **Age/occupation:** e.g., 28, marketing manager
- **Goal:** e.g., "build a consistent morning routine"
- **Frustration:** e.g., "tried 5 apps, none stuck"
- **Where she hangs out:** e.g., Instagram, productivity blogs
- **What would make her try:** e.g., "a friend's recommendation"

### Part 2: Positioning Statement (20 min)

Fill in this template for your product:

> For **[target user]** who **[need]**, **[product name]** is a **[category]** that **[key benefit]**. Unlike **[competitor]**, we **[differentiator]**.

Example: "For busy professionals who need consistent habits, Streak is a habit tracker that builds motivation through visual progress. Unlike Habitica, we focus on simplicity over gamification."

### Part 3: Channel Strategy (45 min)

List 5 possible channels to reach your persona. For each, rate:

| Channel | Cost | Effort | Expected reach | Fit (1-5) |
|---------|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| Instagram ads | $$ | Low | High | ? |
| Product Hunt launch | Free | High | Medium | ? |
| Blog posts / SEO | Free | High | Slow but steady | ? |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Pick your **top 2 channels** and explain why.

### Part 4: Write One Landing Page (60 min)

Write the copy for a simple landing page:
- **Headline** (6-10 words that capture the benefit)
- **Subheadline** (one sentence expanding on the headline)
- **3 bullet points** of key benefits
- **One testimonial** (make it up, but make it realistic)
- **Call to action** (what should the user do next?)

# Quest 8 — AgTech & Food Security

**Sphere:** S | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

A regional government is worried about food supply chain resilience after recent disruptions. They want a brief on where the vulnerabilities are and where technology could help. You're the analyst.

---

## Theory Bite

AgTech and food security sit at the intersection of **biology, logistics, data, and policy**. Key concepts:

- Food systems are global but impact is local
- The biggest problems aren't always about production — they're about distribution and waste
- Technology in agriculture ranges from sensors and drones to supply chain software
- Food security = availability, access, utilization, stability (FAO definition)

---

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: Food System Map (45 min)

Map the journey of one food item (e.g., wheat → bread) from farm to table. Identify:
- 5 stages in the chain
- Where waste happens at each stage
- Where technology could reduce waste

### Part 2: Vulnerability Assessment (30 min)

List 3 risks to the food supply chain in your region:
- Climate-related
- Logistics-related
- Economic/political

Rate each: likelihood (1-5) × impact (1-5) = risk score

### Part 3: Tech Opportunity Brief (45 min)

Pick one vulnerability and write a brief:
- The problem (2 sentences)
- Current approach (what's being done now)
- Tech opportunity (what could technology do differently?)
- One metric for success

### Part 4: Urban Farming Mini-Plan (30 min)

Design a small urban farming setup (balcony, rooftop, or community garden). Choose:
- 3 crops suitable for your climate
- Growing method (containers, raised beds, hydroponics)
- Estimated yield and cost

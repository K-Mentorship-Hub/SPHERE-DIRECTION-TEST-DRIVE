# Quest 7 — Green / Sustainability

**Sphere:** S | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

Your city wants to reduce its carbon footprint by 20% in 5 years. They've hired you as a sustainability consultant to find the three highest-impact actions they can take. You have no budget for research — just publicly available data and your analytical mind.

---

## Theory Bite

Sustainability work is about **understanding systems and finding leverage points**. The core skill:

- Map the problem (where do emissions come from?)
- Find the biggest leverage (what change has the most impact?)
- Navigate trade-offs (what's politically feasible? economically viable?)
- Communicate clearly (numbers > feelings)

Key truth: **the biggest impact often comes from boring systemic changes, not flashy green tech.** Insulation beats solar panels. Public transit beats EV subsidies.

---

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: Emissions Breakdown (30 min)

Research: what are the top 5 sources of carbon emissions in a typical mid-sized city? Create a simple pie chart or table showing approximate percentages.

### Part 2: Leverage Analysis (45 min)

For each of the top 3 sources, list:
- One **high-impact, low-cost** intervention
- One **high-impact, high-cost** intervention
- One **low-impact, popular** intervention (greenwashing alert)

### Part 3: Policy Brief (60 min)

Write a 1-page policy brief for the city council:
- **The problem** (2-3 sentences with data)
- **Three recommended actions** (ranked by impact)
- **Expected reduction** per action
- **One paragraph** addressing the main objection you'd expect

### Part 4: Personal Carbon Audit (30 min)

Calculate your own approximate carbon footprint using an online calculator (search "carbon footprint calculator"). Note your top 3 emission sources and one realistic change you could make.

# Quest 14 — People-Intensive (Human Factor)

**Sphere:** Bridge | **Time:** 2-3 hours | **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly

---

## Scenario

A remote team of 8 people has been together for 6 months. Productivity is dropping, two people haven't spoken in weeks, and the manager doesn't know what's wrong. You're brought in as a team health consultant.

---

## Theory Bite

People-intensive work is about **making groups of humans function well together**. The core skills:

- Reading interpersonal dynamics (what's not being said?)
- Designing processes that reduce friction (meetings, feedback loops, decision-making)
- Facilitating difficult conversations
- Preventing burnout and maintaining motivation
- Building psychological safety (can people speak up without fear?)

Key truth: **most team problems are not about skills — they're about trust, clarity, and communication.**

---

## Hands-On Task

### Part 1: Team Health Checklist (30 min)

Create a team health checklist with 10 yes/no questions. Examples:
- "Does everyone know what the team's top priority is this week?"
- "Has everyone spoken in the last team meeting?"
- "Do people feel safe giving negative feedback?"

Score: 8-10 yes = healthy, 5-7 = needs attention, below 5 = intervention needed.

### Part 2: Meeting Audit (30 min)

Think about the worst meeting you've attended. Diagnose it:
- Was there a clear purpose?
- Did everyone have a chance to speak?
- Was there a decision at the end?
- Did it run over time?

Design a **meeting protocol** that would have made it 50% better. Keep it under 5 rules.

### Part 3: Conflict Resolution Script (45 min)

Write a script for facilitating a conversation between two team members who disagree. Include:
- Opening (set the tone)
- Each person shares their perspective (rules for listening)
- Finding common ground
- Agreeing on next steps

This is harder than it sounds — the script needs to be neutral and not take sides.

### Part 4: Burnout Prevention Plan (30 min)

Create a one-page burnout prevention plan for a remote team:
- 3 warning signs to watch for
- 3 preventive habits (weekly/monthly)
- 1 recovery protocol (what to do when someone is already burned out)

<details>
<summary>Need inspiration for the meeting protocol?</summary>

The "Lean Coffee" format:
1. Anyone posts a topic they want to discuss
2. Group votes on priority (dot voting)
3. Discuss top topic for 5 minutes
4. Vote: continue (5 more min) or move on
5. Repeat until time is up

The "Consent vs. Consensus" rule:
- Don't ask "does everyone agree?" (consensus — slow)
- Ask "does anyone object?" (consent — fast)
- If no objection, proceed

</details>
